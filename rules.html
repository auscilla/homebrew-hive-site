Developer Guidelines & Submission Rules

🎨 Championing Genuine Artistry & Wholesome Entertainment
At Homebrew Hive, we believe retro gaming should celebrate creativity, craftsmanship, and community. Our physical store kiosks showcase games that inspire gamers of all ages, highlighting authentic indie talent and uplifting human artistry.

💡 1. Original Intellectual Property

Submissions must consist of original storylines, characters, world-building, and code. Developers must possess full distribution rights to their ROMs and assets.

🚫 2. No ROM Hacks or Reskins

Games cannot be simple visual or audio reskins of existing commercial titles. We do not accept modified ROMs or unauthorized asset swaps of proprietary games.

🎭 3. No Parody or Trademark Infringement

Games must not rely on parodies or unauthorized uses of trademarked names, logos, or copyrighted characters from established franchises.

🛡️ 4. Wholesome Narrative Focus

Storylines and mechanics cannot center around sexually explicit content, gratuitous erotica, or adult themes unsuitable for public family retail environments.

🤝 5. Zero Tolerance for Hate Content

Narratives, dialogue, or themes promoting violence, hostility, or discrimination toward any race, religion, background, or identity are strictly prohibited.

⚡ 6. Playable & Stable Build

Submitted builds must be stable and fully playable end-to-end without game-breaking bugs, progression blockers, or malicious execution code.

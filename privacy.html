Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Homebrew Hive is run by Aaron Uscilla, a sole proprietor in Florida.

What we collect

  • Accounts: your name, email, a 4-digit PIN, your wishlist, and the games you've printed.
  • Forms: if you contact us, your name, email, and message. If a shop asks for a kiosk, we collect the shop name, city and state, contact name, email, and phone.
  • Developers: game details and the file links you submit.
  • Kiosk prints: when you print a game at a kiosk, we save your email with that print.
  • Sticker form: if you claim a free sticker, we collect your name, mailing address, email, and the game, shop, and purchase date, and use them to send your sticker.

What we don't collect: payment information. The store handles your payment.

How we use it: to run your account, show your wishlist and purchases, review and list games, answer messages, and set up kiosks. We don't sell your information.

Who sees it: our website and database service providers, only to run the site. Shops see their kiosk's print history but not customer names or emails. We'll also share information if the law requires it.

Cookies: we don't use analytics or advertising cookies. If you log in, your browser stores only what's needed to keep you signed in.

Keeping and deleting: we keep your information while your account is open. To delete it, email tryhomebrewhive@gmail.com.

Children: Homebrew Hive isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes: we'll post any updates here with a new date.

Contact: tryhomebrewhive@gmail.com
